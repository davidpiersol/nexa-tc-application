## Global Admin Wiki

Global Admins can open **/admin/global/wiki** to view a centralized index of in-app guidance.

### What it includes

1. Role-oriented help guides.
2. Route-oriented operational notes.
3. Slug identifiers for support and QA references.

### Operational use

1. Use wiki entries during onboarding.
2. Click an article row to open that guide in the Help panel.
3. Keep article content current whenever workflow behavior changes.
