## Welcome to your NEXA workspace

NEXA is the shared table for a transaction—one place for documents, milestones, and messages instead of a dozen threads. You’ll only see what your role and participation allow; that’s by design, not a glitch.

### How to get oriented

1. Open **Help** (the **?** button) on any workspace screen to read guidance for where you are.
2. Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) anytime to search across every article—fast when you’re new.

> **Remember:** Your coordinator still sets brokerage policy—software carries out what your office trains people to do.

### Roles at a glance

- **Transaction coordinators** — Pipeline, documents, checklists, signatures.
- **Agents** — Client-facing updates scoped to their deals.
- **Buyers & sellers** — Plain-language milestones and uploads—not internal chatter.
- **Mortgage & title** — Condition-driven lanes that pair with your processor workflow.

If something feels missing, ask whether **your invite matches the work you’re doing**—scope beats clutter.
