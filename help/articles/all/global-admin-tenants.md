## Global Admin Tenant Management

Use the Tenants page to review tenant records and manage platform-controlled tenant administration.

### What to check

1. Tenant identity, slug, status, and seat limits.
2. Assigned tenant-admin users.
3. Tenant-admin candidates for controlled access changes.

### Important guardrail

Global admins can manage tenants across the platform. Tenant admins remain scoped to their own tenant and should not see global tenant controls.
