## Messaging on the Transaction File

Messaging inside NEXA keeps conversations **tied to the deal**—better than lost SMS threads when memories diverge three weeks before closing.

### Sending a message

1. Open your transaction workspace.
2. Go to **Messages** or **Activity**—your tenant’s label may vary.
3. Write clearly—**subject facts**, **dates**, **who owns next step**.
4. Send. Assume brokerage retention applies—warmth is welcome; gossip isn’t.

> **Tip:** If wires or identities are discussed, switch to phone **using verified numbers**—never instruction swaps inside chat.

### What belongs here vs email

1. **Here:** Timeline updates, doc confirmations, neutral milestone chatter tied to this property.
2. **Outside:** Anything requiring encryption beyond policy—or privileged attorney discussion.

### Tone that earns TC trust

1. Lead with **what changed** and **what you need**.
2. Acknowledge receipts—even “Got it—will upload revised HOA tonight.”

### When emotions spike

1. Pause—draft offline if needed—then post calm logistics.
2. Loop your TC early when parties threaten to walk—facts travel faster than reputational fires.

> **Gold habit:** Close loops explicitly—“Uploaded amended RR—please reload disclosure packet.”
