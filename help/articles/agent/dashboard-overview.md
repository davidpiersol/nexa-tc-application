## Agent Workspace — Stay Ahead of the Next Question

Your workspace mirrors **your** transactions—not the brokerage’s entire roster. Use it to answer clients confidently without chasing files across apps.

### What you’ll see first

1. **Deal headline** — Address, stage language your TC uses, and what matters next—often dates buyers actually ask about.
2. **Documents shared with you** — Anything marked visible for agents—or uploaded under your role—depends on brokerage setup.
3. **Messages on this file** — Keep tone professional; assume compliance retention applies.

> **Tip:** If something looks missing, your TC may not have published it to agent visibility yet—ask before forwarding screenshots.

### Daily rhythm that works

1. Glance milestones **before** calls—not mid-call when surprises sting.
2. Upload disclosures or amendments **the moment they’re final PDFs**—scans beat promises.
3. Confirm receipt when clients upload homework—the psychological win matters.

### Working cleanly with your TC

1. Prefer updates inside NEXA over rogue texts—threads tied to the file age better at dispute time.
2. Name uploads predictably (`Smith_DisclosurePackage.pdf`), not `scan.pdf`.

> **Gold tone:** Agents win repeat business when clients feel **guided**, not **processed**.
