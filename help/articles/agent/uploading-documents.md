## Uploading Documents as an Agent

Your uploads should arrive **labeled**, **complete**, and **timely**—TC teams forgive delays faster than mysteries.

### Upload steps

1. Open your transaction’s **Documents** area from your workspace navigation.
2. Choose **Upload** (or drag files where supported).
3. Pick the **category** that tells the truth—contract, disclosure, inspection, HOA, etc.
4. Confirm the tile appears with today’s date—proof it landed.

> **Remember:** Categories aren’t decorative—they decide who sees what downstream.

### Before you hit upload

1. Rotate scans upright—nobody should crane-neck PDFs at COE.
2. Combine related exhibits **once**—five attachments beat fifty screenshots unless counsel wants separation.

### When buyers or sellers send you photos

1. Convert to **PDF** where brokerage policy requires archival formats.
2. Strip sensitive chatter from email bodies—attach facts, not drama.

### After upload

1. Ping your TC only when policy asks—or when the packet changes material facts (price, dates, parties).
2. If status stays **requested**, ask whether the TC needs a different category or clearer naming—solve logistics before escalating emotion.

> **Warm professionalism:** When something’s rejected, fix naming or completeness—not personalities.
