# Agent CRM Workspace

The agent CRM uses the same personal-work model as the TC CRM while keeping records scoped to the signed-in agent.

## What to check first

1. **Upcoming Actions** for what needs attention now.
2. **Tasks** and **Reminders** for follow-up commitments.
3. **Touches** and **Notes** for recent relationship history.
4. **Connections** for the status of future external CRM links.

## What to do on the page

1. Start on **Upcoming Actions** and clear overdue follow-ups before new outreach.
2. Add a **Task** when there is work to complete; add a **Reminder** when you simply need a prompt at a later date.
3. Record **Touches** after calls, texts, emails, or meetings so the next conversation starts with context.
4. Use **Notes** for private relationship detail that should not disappear into text threads.
5. Use **Relationships** and **Segments** to organize who matters, why they matter, and how warm the relationship is.
6. Review **Connections** only for integration status; external CRM sync is not active until approved setup exists.

## Section-by-section guidance

- **Upcoming**: work overdue first, then today, then this week so urgent follow-up does not get buried by long-range plans.
- **Tasks**: use when someone expects a concrete action from you. Open the detail view before editing so you confirm the right person and due date.
- **Reminders**: use when you need a future nudge but there is no larger task to manage.
- **Touches**: record the contact method, result, and next action after each meaningful conversation.
- **Notes**: preserve private context you may need next time, even when no follow-up date is attached.
- **Relationships**: link people who matter to one another, such as referral sources, family members, or business partners.
- **Segments**: keep SOI, hot/warm/cold, vendor, broker client, and prospect labels useful enough to drive follow-up behavior.
- **Calendar**: scan due work by date when a list view is not enough.
- **Import / Export**: use only when you are intentionally moving contact data in or out.
- **Connections**: review provider status; do not assume a listed CRM is already syncing.

## Good daily rhythm

1. Open CRM at the start of the workday.
2. Clear overdue items before beginning new outreach.
3. Log every meaningful touch while it is still fresh.
4. Add the next task or reminder before you leave the record.
5. Revisit the week and month buckets before ending the day so future work does not quietly become overdue.

## Contact behavior

Contacts remain canonical in Choral Point. CRM activity can link to an existing contact or broker, but CRM does not create a second people directory.

## Privacy boundary

Your CRM workspace is user-specific within the tenant. Another agent or TC should not see your private CRM tasks, reminders, touches, notes, or relationships merely because you share a company account.
