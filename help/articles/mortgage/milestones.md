## Loan milestones that partners actually notice

Milestones translate underwriting gates into **human schedules**—borrowers pack boxes around them; TCs align signatures accordingly.

### Typical milestone ladder

1. **Application / disclosures signed** — Ethics moment—borrowers must understand what they’re signing.
2. **Underwriting review** — Silent marathon—communicate rhythm so silence doesn’t read neglect.
3. **Conditional approval** — Condition lists become choreography sheets—owners assigned per item.
4. **Clear to close** — Celebrate quietly—still verify wires verbally before trusting templates.

> **Gold overlay:** Each milestone deserves **one narrative owner**—otherwise borrowers hear harmonic discord.

### Communicating delays

1. Lead with **facts**, **cause**, **next action window**.
2. Avoid blaming coworkers wholesale—internal accountability stays internal.

### With agents & TCs

1. Milestones broadcast outward **when helpful**—suppress noise when underwriting breathes internally.

> **Mindset:** Milestones aren’t trophies—they’re promises—keep language humane when dates breathe.
