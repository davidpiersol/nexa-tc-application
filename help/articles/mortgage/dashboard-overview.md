## Mortgage workspace — read the deal once, chase conditions twice as smart

Your dashboard reflects **this borrower’s file**—conditions, milestones, and paperwork lanes scoped to processing—not marketing fluff.

### Opening posture

1. Scan **outstanding conditions** before inbound calls—borrowers feel cared for when you reference specifics immediately.
2. Track **document statuses**—requested vs received saves underwriting rework.

> **Tip:** When LOS differs from NEXA labels, follow LOS truth—but mirror narratives here so agents hear one coherent story.

### Pace that honors underwriting reality

1. Bundle asks **once** when possible—scattershot pings fray realtor relationships.
2. Timestamp uploads mentally—“received Friday EOD” beats ambiguous silence.

### Tone across parties

1. With borrowers—warm clarity about **what** and **why**.
2. With TCs—numbered asks referencing loan milestones—professional shorthand saves cycles.

> **Gold habit:** When docs reject—explain **remediation path**, not just denial codes.
