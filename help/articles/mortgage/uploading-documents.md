## Uploading lender-critical documents

Processors survive on legibility and naming discipline—give them PDFs that behave.

### Upload discipline

1. Pull borrower uploads **into underwriting-ready PDFs**—rotate, merge logically, label consistently (`Smith_2023_W2.pdf`).
2. Upload under categories your tenant exposes—**income**, **assets**, **credit explanations**, etc.—truth beats guessing.

> **Remember:** Partial uploads stall underwriting twice—send complete packets when policy allows.

### Sensitive handling

1. Never chase SS snippets through unsecured SMS—use sanctioned borrower portals or tracked uploads here when configured.
2. Redact what regulation demands—don’t over-share unrelated bank pages “just in case.”

### After upload

1. Note LOS received timestamps internally—but mirror borrower reassurance (“Docs safely received—expect underwriting refresh Tuesday”).
2. When rejecting files—state **exact replacement needed**.

### Coordinating with TCs

1. Reference milestone gates plainly—“Clear to close blocked pending HOA resale PDF.”
2. Celebrate cleared conditions succinctly—morale lubricates busy pipelines.

> **Warm professionalism:** Borrowers mirror your calm—or your panic—choose wisely.
