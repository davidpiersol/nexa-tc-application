## Important dates to keep in view

Dates aren’t trivia—they’re handshakes between **your contract**, **your lender**, and **closing**. You don’t need to memorize everything—just know **which dates deserve your attention**.

### Dates buyers often watch

1. **Inspection window** — When you can learn the home’s condition and negotiate thoughtfully.
2. **Loan milestones** — When paperwork must land so underwriting stays on rhythm.
3. **Closing day** — When ownership transfers—often refined as you approach wire instructions.

> **Remember:** “Closing date” can adjust when repairs or underwriting require breathing room—your agent explains actuality vs hope.

### How to stay calm around deadlines

1. Put reminders on **your** calendar when your coordinator highlights them—not every line item needs an alarm.
2. Ask **what happens if** once—then trust the plan unless facts change.

### If you’ll miss something

Tell your agent **early**—early honesty preserves choices; last-minute surprises shrink them.

### Weekend grace

Real humans close deals—if something falls on a holiday weekend, expect humane adjustments.

> **You’re not behind** if you’re asking questions—you’re participating.
