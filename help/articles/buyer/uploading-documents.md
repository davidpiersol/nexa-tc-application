## Uploading documents when asked

Sometimes your lender or coordinator needs **your** paperwork—a bank letter, a letter explaining a deposit, or a clear photo of ID when policy allows. Uploading here keeps everything attached to **your file**, so nothing wanders into the wrong inbox.

### Simple steps

1. Open the message or reminder from your coordinator—it usually tells you **what** to upload.
2. Tap **Upload** (or follow the link they send).
3. Choose a **clear photo or PDF**—not a blurry screenshot if you can avoid it.
4. Submit once—if you’re unsure it went through, ask your coordinator to confirm receipt.

### Photos that work well

1. Use **bright, even light**—kitchen counters near a window beat car dashboards.
2. Frame the **whole page**—all four corners visible if it’s a physical document.

### What not to upload unless asked

Avoid sending extra bank statements or letters “just in case”—your coordinator will ask precisely what underwriting needs.

> **Privacy:** Only upload through links **your team gave you**—don’t email sensitive documents to random addresses.

### If you feel stuck

Call your **agent**—they’ve walked hundreds of buyers through this moment.
