## Signing documents online

When it’s time to sign, you’ll receive an email with a secure link. That link belongs to **you**—don’t forward it to friends or post it online.

### Your signing steps

1. Open the email on **your phone or computer**—use something you’re comfortable reading carefully on.
2. Tap **Review documents**.
3. Move page by page—read anything new since your last conversation with your agent.
4. Sign where fields appear—often initials on notices and a full signature where your purchase agreement expects it.

> **Remember:** Read dollar amounts, dates, and names slowly—even familiar-looking forms deserve fresh eyes.

### If something looks wrong

1. **Stop** before signing.
2. Call **your agent** or **coordinator** using the phone number you already trust—not a number from a surprise text.

### After you sign

1. You’ll usually get confirmation—keep it like you’d keep a receipt.
2. Your team gets notified automatically—no need to screenshot unless someone asks.

### Phones and tablets

Signing works on mobile—just give yourself quiet minutes so you don’t rush initials.

> **Warm reassurance:** Millions of buyers sign this way—take breaths between pages if it feels like a lot.
