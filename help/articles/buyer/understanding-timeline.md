## Understanding your timeline

A purchase timeline isn’t a promise that nothing will shift—it’s a **shared picture** of what usually happens next so nobody feels alone guessing.

### How to read milestones

1. **Big moments** — Inspections, loan approval milestones, title commitment reviews—your coordinator labels what matters in **your** market.
2. **Quiet periods** — Sometimes nothing flashy happens while underwriting reads files—**quiet isn’t delay**.

> **Comfort:** Silence usually means careful work behind the scenes—not forgetting you.

### When dates move

Inspection repairs, appraisal gaps, or seller responses can nudge closing—your agent explains **why**, not just **when**.

### Your role

1. Return calls within the windows you promise.
2. Upload homework **early** when asked—deadlines feel softer when you beat them.

### Emotional honesty

If anxiety spikes, tell your agent **what you’re worried about**—money, timing, repairs—in plain words. They translate fear into next steps.

> **Warm truth:** Timelines breathe—teams who communicate early keep surprises kind.
