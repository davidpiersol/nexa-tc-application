## Welcome—we’re glad you’re here

Buying a home is a big chapter. This portal is simply **your private corner of the journey**—where your team can share what matters for **your** purchase, without handing you the entire office’s to-do list.

### What this space is for

1. Seeing **what’s next**—milestones your coordinator wants you to notice.
2. **Uploading** documents when asked—bank letters, IDs (when permitted), or disclosures your lender needs.
3. **Signing** agreements electronically when your coordinator sends them—quickly and securely.

### What you won’t see here

You won’t browse other people’s homes or paperwork—your view belongs to **your transaction alone**. That protects your privacy and keeps everyone focused.

### How to feel settled using it

1. Bookmark this link once your coordinator says it’s yours.
2. Check in when they ask—or when you receive an email saying something needs attention.
3. Ask questions out loud to **your agent or coordinator** when anything feels unclear—we’d rather explain twice than guess wrong once.

> **A gentle note:** If something looks urgent in email but calm here, trust your coordinator’s guidance—they sometimes sequence messages so nothing noisy wakes you at midnight.

### We’re on your side

This isn’t about software—it’s about **clarity** between **you**, **your agent**, and everyone helping close calmly.
