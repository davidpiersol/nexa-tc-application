## Disclosures—the honesty layer buyers deserve

Disclosures aren’t punishment—they’re **good faith** about what living in your home has honestly been like.

### Why completeness matters

1. Buyers make choices based on what they read—surprises later hurt trust—and sometimes contracts.
2. Your agent coaches **what belongs** in your market—listen closely.

### Typical themes you might touch

1. **HOA or condo quirks** — Assessments, rentals rules, parking realities.
2. **Systems** — Roof age, HVAC context—not poetic, just factual.

> **Warm candor:** Accuracy protects **your** closing day calm—not just the buyer’s.

### How uploads usually flow

1. Your coordinator or agent tells you **what format** they need—PDF scans beat cellphone novels when legibility counts.
2. Upload once you’ve reviewed with your agent—**slow signatures beat frantic corrections**.

### If something embarrasses you

Tell your agent privately—facts framed kindly prevent courtroom tones later.

> **Remember:** Disclosure forms aren’t confessional essays—they’re factual inventories buyers rely on.
