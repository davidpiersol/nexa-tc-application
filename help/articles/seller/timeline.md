## Your selling timeline—steady beats frantic

Timelines tell **what tends to happen next**—not that nothing will ever slide a few days when inspections speak loudly.

### Milestones you’ll hear about

1. **Listing activation** — Photos live, disclosure packets align.
2. **Under contract** — Buyer earnest money and timelines begin dancing together.
3. **Toward closing** — Title, payoff statements, final walk expectations—your coordinator guides specifics.

> **Grace:** Quiet weeks sometimes mean careful underwriting—not forgetting your sale.

### When buyers ask for repairs

Your agent translates requests into strategy—you choose informed calm, not midnight panic.

### Closing date flexibility

Life happens—sometimes closing shifts when lenders or repairs need air. Your agent explains **why** so **meaning** travels with **dates**.

### Emotional stamina

Pack boxes gradually—celebrate small completions—invite friends for porch tea while paperwork hums elsewhere.

> **Warm truth:** Sellers who pace themselves finish proud—not depleted.
