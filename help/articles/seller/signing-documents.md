## Signing as a seller

Signing days arrive quietly—a link in email, a few minutes at your kitchen table—and suddenly progress moves.

### Your calm checklist

1. Open the link **when you can focus**, not mid-school-drop-off unless you must.
2. Read dollar figures and dates aloud once—ears catch mistakes eyes skim.
3. Sign each labelled spot—initials where initials belong, full signatures where contracts demand.

> **Kind reminder:** If buyer credits or dates shifted since yesterday’s conversation, pause—call your agent before ink lands digitally.

### Phones are fine

Signing works on mobile—still gift yourself enough light to read comfortably.

### After signing

You’ll usually receive confirmation—keep it like you’d keep any major receipt.

### When nerves spike

That’s normal—homes hold memories. Breathe, read slowly, call your agent if meaning—not mechanics—feels fuzzy.

> **You’re still human** behind digital signatures—humans double-check.
