# Billing and Invoices

Use **Billing** to create receivables for TC work, MLS-only jobs, and other approved services.

## Billing workspace

1. Use **Billing** first when you want the quick answer: how much was billed, how much came in, what tax is tied to received money, and what needs follow-up.
2. Use **Invoices** when you need to find a specific invoice, sort the working list, print multiple invoices, or open one record.
3. Use **Reports** when you need period totals and drill-down detail rather than invoice maintenance.

## Creating an invoice

1. Choose **Create invoice** from Billing.
2. Select the service type that matches the work performed.
3. Start typing the broker or client name; choose the matching contact when one exists.
4. Link either the transaction source or the MLS-only source when the invoice belongs to one of those records.
5. Review the description, quantity, unit amount, and tax amount before saving.
6. Save. Choral Point takes you straight to the invoice detail page so you can review what was created.

## Invoice detail

The detail page is read-only until you choose **Edit**. Use:

- **Preview Invoice** to inspect the formatted invoice.
- **Download PDF** for the clean invoice artifact.
- **Email** when the recipient and provider path are configured.

Invoices default to **payable upon receipt** and support reminder tracking for unpaid balances.

## What to do on the invoice list

1. Search by invoice number, billed person, or another visible invoice detail.
2. Sort the list when you are working oldest first, chasing balances, or reviewing recent billing.
3. Open an invoice to inspect it before sending or changing it.
4. Multi-select invoices only when you intend to print a batch.

## What to follow up

1. Review due-today, 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day reminders.
2. Use the dashboard reminder modal to decide who needs a payment follow-up.
3. When payment arrives, update the invoice so reports and tax-on-received totals stay truthful.

## Tax note

Taxes are calculated from the configured TC setting. Confirm the applicable local GRT rate before sending if the property or service location changes the tax treatment.
