# MLS Entry Workspace

Use **MLS entry** for listing work that is not a full transaction-coordination file.

## What belongs here

1. MLS-only jobs requested by a broker.
2. Property and seller details needed to prepare a listing.
3. Internal notes, billing context, and submission readiness.

## How the workflow works now

1. Open **MLS entry** to review existing MLS-only jobs.
2. Choose **New MLS entry** when a broker has asked you to list a property without managing the full transaction.
3. Fill the request information first: which broker asked, service type, property type, and the seller/property basics.
4. Move through the form sections and complete everything you know from the broker packet or source listing information.
5. Save the job before leaving the page so the request can be tracked and billed even if the final MLS portal entry still happens outside Choral Point.

## On the list page

1. Open a job when you need to review what was captured or confirm its current status.
2. Use the job detail as the working record for what was requested, what is still missing, and which broker should be billed.
3. Use **MLS Write Access** only when you need the research explanation for why direct submit is not yet turned on.

## What to check before you call it ready

1. The requesting broker is correct.
2. The property type and service type are correct.
3. Seller and property information match the source material.
4. Any fields still handled only in the MLS portal are noted before you bill or hand off the work.

## Important boundary

The current form is scaffolding, not a certified SWMLS entry form. Final required fields, conditional rules, and any write-back path must come from GAAR/SWMLS/FlexMLS before the app can replace portal entry safely.

> **Practical rule:** If the MLS portal requires a field that Choral Point does not yet show, capture it in the source system and flag the gap for the field-model backlog.
