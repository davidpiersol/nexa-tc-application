## Contacts Directory

Use **Contacts** as the canonical CRM surface for people and relationship metadata.

### What belongs here

1. Name details (salutation, first, middle, last, suffix).
2. Contact details (email, phone, company).
3. Address details (street, city, state, ZIP, country).
4. Categories (sorted; `Other` includes a required description).

### Why this matters

1. Intake and reporting should reference canonical contacts rather than retyping names.
2. CRM quality improves when records stay normalized and reusable.
3. Category consistency makes filtering, automation, and scorecards more reliable.

### Workflow

1. Open **Contacts**.
2. Search first to avoid duplicates.
3. Use **Add contacts** for new records.
4. Open a contact to review, edit, or delete (delete always requires warning confirmation).
