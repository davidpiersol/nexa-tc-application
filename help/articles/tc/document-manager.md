## Managing Transaction Documents

Documents are the backbone of your file. Keep names predictable, categories honest, and signatures traceable—your title partner will thank you at wire.

### Upload methods

You’ll usually see one or more paths—depending on how your brokerage configured NEXA:

1. **Direct upload** — From the transaction **Documents** tab, choose **Upload**, pick category, attach the PDF or image.
2. **Drag-and-drop** — When your browser supports it, drop files onto the upload zone—same category rules apply.
3. **Email-in** — Some offices route a transaction-specific inbox (for example `deal+...@yourdomain`) into NEXA. Check with your admin—subject lines and routing rules matter.

> **Note:** Email-in is powerful—also noisy. Train agents to include the transaction reference your policy requires.

### Category organization

Categories are how everyone finds things later—**contract**, **disclosure**, **title**, **mortgage**, **inspection**, **HOA**, **other**.

1. Pick the closest truth—don’t stash disclosures under “other” because it’s faster today.
2. If you must use **other**, add a bright filename (`Smith_COE_Addendum_v2.pdf`), not `scan001.pdf`.

### Approving and rejecting

When reviews are enabled:

1. Open the tile—scan who uploaded it and when.
2. Move status from **uploaded** toward **under review**, then **approved** when brokerage standards are met—reject with a clear note back to the sender.

> **Warm professionalism:** Rejections land better with **what’s missing** and **who fixes it**—not just “noncompliant.”

### Sending for signature

From the manager (when DocuSign—or your e-sign—is wired):

1. Choose the prepared PDF or packet.
2. Launch your signing workflow—see **Sending Documents for Signature** for signer order and envelope hygiene.

### Version history

Treat uploads as progressive truth:

1. Prefer **new uploads with clear version cues** (`Purchase_v3_final.pdf`) rather than overwriting silently where your system tracks revisions.
2. When wire instructions change—**new PDF**, new acknowledgment—even if it feels bureaucratic.

> **Gold standard:** One authoritative contract PDF per negotiation phase beats five ambiguous scans named “final_final.”
