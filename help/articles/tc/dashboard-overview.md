## Your TC Dashboard — At a Glance

This is your morning coffee view: what’s open, what’s due, and what needs your eye today—without digging through email threads.

## Stats cards

Across the top, four numbers tell you where fire drills tend to show up:

1. **Active transactions** — Files that aren’t closed yet. Use this as your working inventory count.
2. **Due this week** — Tasks with due dates landing in the next seven days. Treat this as your short-range radar.
3. **Pending reviews** — Anything waiting on eyes—documents under review, First Pass items, or similar—depending on how your office wires NEXA.
4. **Signatures needed** — Agreements or packets still waiting on DocuSign (or your configured e-sign path).

> **Remember:** These figures reflect **your tenant and what you can access**—not the whole MLS.

## Pipeline kanban

Your pipeline reads left to right: early-stage deals move toward closing.

1. Scan columns (**Pre-listing → Active listing → Under contract → Pending → Closed**) for stranded files.
2. Drag a card when the deal truly advances stages—your brokerage sets policy on who moves stages when.

The thin accent bar on each card hints at stage; the gold progress strip reflects checklist completion where checklists exist.

> **Tip:** If a card jumps back after you move it, refresh once—a save didn’t stick (often permissions or session).

## Deadline color coding

In the **Deadlines** list, the colored dots are simple urgency cues:

- **Gold** — Due soon or flagged high-touch by your team’s convention.
- **Navy** — Standard rhythm items—still real deadlines.
- **Warm brown** — Lower urgency reminders—not optional forever, just proportionate.

Your coordinator habits matter here: if everyone ignores soft cues, tighten brokerage SLAs instead of fighting the tool.

## Tasks

Tasks sit beside deadlines so you can clear TC-owned items without opening each file. Checking something off updates the transaction timeline when tasks are linked—otherwise treat tasks as your personal queue.

## Starting something new

When you’re ready to open a file from scratch, use **New Transaction** wherever your brokerage surfaces it—often from **this dashboard** or the **Transactions** list. That button kicks off the guided flow: identifiers (MLS, address), parties, then documents and First Pass—see **Opening a New Transaction** for the full walkthrough.

> **Mindset:** The dashboard rewards rhythm—same glance daily, fewer surprises at COE.
