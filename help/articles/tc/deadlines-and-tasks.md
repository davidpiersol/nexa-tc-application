## Tracking Deadlines and Tasks

Deadlines are promises your file makes to the calendar. Tasks are promises your team makes to each other. Keep both honest.

### How deadlines appear

Depending on configuration:

1. **Contract milestones** may seed automatically from dates you enter during First Pass or manual entry.
2. **Checklist due dates** surface in the dashboard **Deadlines** rail when items carry dates.
3. **Manual deadlines** may be added where your tenant exposes that control—use sparingly and label clearly.

> **Tip:** If something looks early or late, verify **calendar math** against your contract—software doesn’t interpret “business days” unless someone configured it.

### Color coding (dashboard)

Match what you learned on the dashboard overview:

- **Gold** — closer horizon or higher sensitivity.
- **Navy** — steady cadence work.
- **Warm brown** — softer reminders—still real.

Use color as triage, not permission to ignore “brown” items through closing.

### Adding custom deadlines

When your UI allows:

1. Anchor each deadline to a **named milestone**—not “ASAP.”
2. Attach an owner—TC, agent, title—so everyone knows who moves it.

### Task board behavior

Tasks on the TC dashboard aggregate items across files where relevant:

1. Complete tasks when the underlying work is truly done—not when you hope it’s done.
2. If tasks duplicate elsewhere (transaction-level lists), treat **one source as authoritative** per office policy.

### Daily digest email

If your brokerage enables digests:

1. Expect a concise roll-up—due items, signatures pending, key dates.
2. Train assistants not to forward digests externally—they often summarize privileged file facts.

> **Mindset:** Deadlines slip when ownership blurs—pair each date with a human name in your stand-ups.
