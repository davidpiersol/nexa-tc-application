# Reports

Use **Reports** when you need totals, trends, and drill-down detail rather than day-to-day task work.

## Current live reporting

Billing is the first active report family. It provides:

1. Monthly totals.
2. Quarterly totals.
3. Year-end totals.
4. Drill-down rows showing who was billed, when, what was received, and the tax amounts tied to receipts.

## What to do on the Reports page

1. Start with the period card that matches the question you are answering: month, quarter, or year.
2. Compare **total billed**, **total received**, and **taxes on received** before drawing conclusions about cash flow.
3. Open the billing report when you need the underlying invoices instead of just the headline number.
4. Use the invoice detail link when a row looks wrong; fix the source invoice rather than trying to correct a report total directly.

## Planned report families

The report hub is intentionally broader than billing. Future report cards are reserved for contacts, brokers, transactions and listings, and document operations.

## How to use it well

1. Start from the summary period that matches the question.
2. Drill into the invoice detail before making accounting decisions.
3. Treat reports as a review surface; use the source workspace to correct the underlying record.
4. Export or share only after the billing records underneath have been reviewed for the period.
