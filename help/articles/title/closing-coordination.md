## Closing coordination — choreography worth rehearsing

Closing isn’t one appointment—it’s **aligned arrivals**: funded wires, recorded deeds, emotional humans packing cars.

### Before scheduling signing

1. Confirm **curatives cleared** per underwriting/title comfort—not hopeful optimism.
2. Align **notary + cashier’s check / wire** instructions verbally—scammers love rushed Fridays.

> **Critical:** Wire instructions **never** debut solely via chat—voice-verify using trusted numbers already on file.

### Day-of rhythm

1. Arrive documents stacked logically—buyers sign smoother when pagination flows naturally.
2. Hold emotional space—first-time sellers cry; first-time buyers shake—both deserve dignity.

### Post-closing kindness

1. Confirm recording milestones outward where appropriate—silence after signing breeds midnight fears.

### When wires delay

1. Narrate transparently—facts calm faster than silence wearing perfume.

> **Warm closure:** People remember **how** you landed the plane—not just that wheels touched runway.
