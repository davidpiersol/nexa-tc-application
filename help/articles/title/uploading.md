## Uploading title-critical documents

Clean uploads shorten examiner reroutes—blurrier scans lengthen everybody’s afternoon.

### Upload habits

1. Orient pages portrait—double-check exhibit stitching order matches recorded references.
2. Label filenames like adults (`Jones_Deed_of_Trust_Recorded.pdf`), not `scan7.pdf`.

> **Gold rule:** Recorded instruments deserve optical clarity—retakes beat apologies.

### Sensitive packets

1. Redact unrelated borrower accounts when sharing lender-required proofs—precision respects privacy law posture.

### Version control

1. When amendments arrive—**new upload**, note superseded gracefully in messaging (“Rev B replaces Rev A uploaded Monday”).

### Aligning with escrow

1. Confirm receipt timestamps internally—borrowers stress less when handshake timing matches reality.

> **Tone:** Upload discipline equals neighbor kindness—every hour saved compounds courtesy.
