## Title checklist discipline

Checklists aren’t bureaucracy—they’re **burglar alarms** on forgotten curatives.

### Working the list

1. Clear items **in dependency order**—some lender approvals gate affidavits logically.
2. Note **who owns** each curative internally—even title teams divide specialties.

> **Remember:** Checking boxes prematurely ages poorly at recording desks—verify artifacts first.

### Exceptions translation

1. Map Schedule B-II entries to **human responsibilities**—borrower vs seller vs HOA contact.
2. Track HOA payoff routing early—seasonal mail slows assumptions.

### Communication escalations

1. Email summaries after verbal alignment—tone stays civil when memories diverge weeks later.

> **Warm professionalism:** Say “we’re staging recording packets” instead of “waiting vaguely.”
